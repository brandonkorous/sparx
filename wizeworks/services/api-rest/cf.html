Contributors

The people who write and photograph The Marrow Review. Pick one to read everything they have done for us.

Rosalind Pike

Editor. Rosalind spent twenty years as a food editor before starting The Marrow Review in 2019, to write about where food comes from rather than what to do with it once it arrives. She lives near Oundle and still keeps the notebooks from every mill she has visited.

Tobias Frankland

Commodities correspondent. Tobias traded grain for a merchant in King's Lynn for eleven years and now writes the Ledger section: prices, contracts, and the weather behind both. He is unusually patient with people who think a futures chart is a forecast.

Amara Boateng

Reporter. Amara covers the Table section, following food from a supplier's yard to a restaurant's pass. Before the Review she reported on city markets for a regional paper, and she still starts every story by asking who unloads the van.

Jean-Luc Marchetti-Owusu

Photographer. Jean-Luc has photographed farms, mills and kitchens for the Review since its first issue. He works with available light wherever he can, and he would rather wait an hour for a field to look like itself than arrange it.
