The four reasons you're about to close this tab.

All of them are good reasons. Here is the honest version of each, including the one where the honest answer is "not yet".

  • "I've just got everything working."

    Then don't move it all at once.

    Start with the one that hurts most — usually bookings or invoices — and leave the rest where it is. Piggles is fifteen apps whether you use one of them or all of them, so there is no penalty for moving slowly and nothing extra to pay when you finish.

  • "Moving the data will be a nightmare."

    Bring it in a spreadsheet. Take it back the same way.

    Customers, products and orders import from the files your current tools already export. And the door swings both ways: everything in Piggles exports whenever you want, in formats other software can actually read. Leaving is not a support ticket.

  • "I'm not technical."

    That is the entire design brief.

    Signing up asks two questions — what the business is called and what you want to start with — and the workspace arrives set up rather than empty. There is nothing in it called a pipeline stage, a record type or a price book unless you go looking for one.

  • "What if I outgrow it?"

    You'd have to outgrow the platform, not the friendly names.

    Wholesale pricing, multiple warehouses, approval rules and automations are all in there, included, waiting for the day you need them. What you would grow out of is the amount of room — more team, more storage — and that is a number you change, not a product you leave.

Questions people actually ask.

The ones that come up before anybody signs anything. Every answer is the one on the pricing and trust pages, not a shorter version of it.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The trial is fourteen days with no card. If you decide not to carry on, nothing happens — there is no charge to cancel before.

What happens if I go over one of the limits?

Nothing you already have is touched, and nothing you are part way through is stopped. You get a quiet notice as you approach it, and the option to add more room in one tap at the moment it matters — with the price on the button, not behind it. If you do nothing, only new additions of that one kind pause. Your website stays up, your customers stay visible, and order confirmations and password resets always go out regardless.

Can I take my data with me if I leave?

All of it, whenever you want, in formats other software can actually read — customers, products, orders, invoices and everything you have written. You do not have to ask, and you do not have to be leaving.

What if I run two businesses?

Each business is its own subscription, with its own website, customers and books kept completely separate. That is deliberate: sharing them is almost always a mistake you find out about at tax time.

Do you use my business data to train AI?

No. Not to train a model, not to improve a shared assistant, not anonymised, not aggregated. Any AI feature runs on a key you connect yourself, which means the data goes where you agreed and nowhere else — and you can revoke it whenever you want.

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Not yet. When there is, it will be a straightforward reduction rather than a different plan with different limits.
