**Version:** 1.0
**Author:** Brandon Korous
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-30

The source for `/compare/*` on meetpiggles.com. Every row was read from the
competitor's own site or help center on 2026-09-30. Brandon approved naming
competitors on these pages (the standing no-names rule is overridden here only).

Rules for the pages built from this file:

- No competitor prices. Structure only ("sold separately", "per seat").
- Say where each one wins, first and plainly.
- A row we could not confirm on an official page is **not confirmed** on the page
  and never shown as "no".
- Re-check every row before changing a page. Date the page with the check date.

Key: **B** built in. **Add-on** sold separately by the same company. **3P** needs
another company's app. **N** not offered. **?** not confirmed.

## Square

Payments and point of sale for shops, restaurants and appointment businesses,
built around its own card readers.

- Wins: its own card hardware (Register, Terminal, Stand, Tap to Pay), in-person
  payments, built-in banking, restaurant and retail depth.
- Plans are per location; Payroll is a separate paid add-on; hardware sold
  separately. https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8569-switch-to-the-new-square-subscription-plans
- Site/store B. POS B. Appointments B (also still sold as Square Appointments
  tiers). Invoices/estimates B. Customer directory B. Email and SMS marketing B
  (texts billed per use past an allowance). Shifts/timecards B. Payroll Add-on.
  Multi-location inventory, vendors, purchase orders B (Square for Retail).
  Forms, blog, help desk ?. Accounting 3P.
- Leaving: customers and item library export to CSV (from Square's community
  answers, not its help center).

## Shopify

An online-store-first platform that also sells in person, on social and on
marketplaces.

- Wins: sales channels and marketplaces, checkout, the largest app store,
  shipping labels, reports, Sidekick AI. https://www.shopify.com/pricing
- Transaction fee on every plan unless you use Shopify Payments. Staff accounts
  capped by plan. POS Pro is an add-on.
- Site/store/blog B. POS B (Pro add-on). Appointments 3P
  (https://apps.shopify.com/collections/appointment-booking-apps). Invoices via
  draft orders. Customer list B. Email/SMS B (Shopify Messaging, usage billed past
  an allowance). Forms B. Automations B (Flow). Multi-location inventory and
  purchase orders B. Staff scheduling, time clock, payroll N or 3P. Help desk 3P.
  Accounting 3P.
- Leaving: products, customers, inventory, discounts and orders export to CSV.
  https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/csv-files

## Wix

A drag-and-drop website builder with business apps on top.

- Wins: design freedom and templates, a large app market, several AI agents.
  https://www.wix.com/business-software
- Plan tiers decide store and marketing features and how many collaborators.
- Site, store, blog, forms, automations B. Bookings B. Invoices and quotes B.
  Contacts and lead pipeline B. Email marketing B. SMS only through automations.
  Social scheduling B. POS B in US, Canada and UK. Multi-location inventory B.
  Purchase orders ?. Time clock and payroll ?. Help desk ?. Accounting ?.
- Leaving: contacts, orders and products export to CSV.
  https://support.wix.com/en/article/exporting-your-contacts

## Squarespace

A design-led website builder for creatives, service businesses and small shops.

- Wins: templates and design polish, AI site generation.
  https://www.squarespace.com/design-intelligence
- Transaction fee on the Basic plan for online sales. Email Campaigns and
  Scheduling (Acuity) are separate products. https://www.squarespace.com/pricing
- Site, blog, forms, members, contacts B. Store B. Invoices B. Proposals B.
  Scheduling Add-on (Acuity). Email Campaigns Add-on. POS through the iOS app,
  US only, needs a Square account and reader. Purchase orders, staff scheduling,
  payroll, help desk not listed. Accounting 3P.
- Leaving: orders, products and customers export to CSV.
  https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206540677-Export-Commerce-orders

## HubSpot (free and Starter)

A CRM-centered platform for marketing, sales and service, sold as Hubs.

- Wins: CRM depth, marketing automation, one CRM under every Hub.
  https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm/starter
- Priced per seat. Revenue Hub (was Commerce Hub) is its own subscription; full
  quotes need it. https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/manage-seats
- Website, landing pages, blog B. CRM B. Email, forms, workflows B. SMS B. Social
  posting B. Tickets and shared inbox B. AI B. Meeting links B. Invoices,
  payments, quotes via Revenue Hub. Online store, POS, inventory, purchase
  orders, staff scheduling, payroll, accounting not found (treated as ?).
- Leaving: records export to CSV or XLSX, plus a full account export.
  https://knowledge.hubspot.com/import-and-export/export-records

## Zoho One

A suite of 45+ business apps under one sign-in.

- Wins: sheer breadth, including real accounting (Books), payroll in some
  countries, HR and a help desk. https://www.zoho.com/one/
- Priced per employee and you license every employee. Payroll only in some
  countries.
- Site B. Store B. Bookings B. Invoices and quotes B. CRM B. Email marketing B.
  Social B. Forms B. Help desk B. Accounting B. HR and payroll B (region-limited).
  Inventory and purchase orders B. Automations and AI ?. POS ?. SMS ?.
- Leaving: each app exports separately; CRM exports to CSV or XLSX.
  https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/export-data/articles/export-crm-data
