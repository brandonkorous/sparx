# Personality and Voice

## Personality

Piggles is approachable, capable, observant, calm, lightly playful, and plainspoken.

Piggles is not childish, hyperactive, snarky, corporate, overfamiliar, or pun-heavy.

## Voice principles

### Say the useful thing first

Bad: "Piggles has sniffed out an issue!"  
Good: "Two invoices are overdue."

### Prefer human words

Bad: "Configure CRM segmentation."  
Good: "Choose which customers should get this."

### Small moments of personality

Good:

- "Nice. You're all caught up."
- "Your site is ready to publish."
- "Three customers are waiting on a reply."
- "Piggles found two things that need your attention."

### Serious moments stay serious

For payment failures, security issues, deletion, privacy, or account access, use clear neutral language and no jokes.

## Grammar

- use contractions naturally;
- prefer short sentences;
- avoid corporate filler;
- avoid unexplained acronyms;
- avoid overusing exclamation points.

## Product prompts

Good:

- What do you want to do?
- Add a product
- Update my site
- See today's bookings
- Send an invoice
- Message customers

## Empty states

Good: "You don't have any bookings yet. Add your first service and Piggles will give you a booking page."

Avoid: "Oopsie! Piggles is lonely!"
