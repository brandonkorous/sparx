# Messaging System

## Primary

**Piggles**  
Business software for people who have a business to run.

## Core promise

Run the business. Piggles helps with the business software.

## Supporting themes

### Powerful without the software vocabulary

You should not need to know what a CRM or CMS is to manage customers or update your site.

### One Piggles

One subscription, the apps you need, without module-price math.

### Your business in one workspace

Site, customers, bookings, stock, invoices, messages, and more can work together.

### Simple doesn't mean basic

Piggles can stay approachable while supporting advanced work.

## Pricing copy

Preferred:

- "Piggles is $49/month."
- "All the Piggles apps are included."
- "Add the tools you want to your workspace. Your price doesn't change just because you turned on Bookings."

Avoid: "Unlock premium modules."

## Meet / Get / My

**Meet Piggles** — discovery and introduction.  
**Get Piggles** — signup and onboarding.  
**My Piggles** — ownership and daily operation.
