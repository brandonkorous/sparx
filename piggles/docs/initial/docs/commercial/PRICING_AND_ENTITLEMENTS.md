# Pricing and Entitlements

## Public pricing target

# Piggles — $49/month

All normal Piggles apps included.

## Commercial philosophy

Charge for **scale**, not access to understandable business functions.

A customer should not ask: "Do I need to pay extra for bookings?"

They should know: "Piggles includes the tools. My bill changes when my business needs more capacity."

## Initial included-capacity hypothesis

- 1 business
- 1 location
- 1 primary site
- 3 users
- 10,000 customer/contact records
- 10–25 GB storage
- 5,000–10,000 email sends/month
- products/services: unlimited or very high
- bookings: unlimited
- invoices: unlimited
- normal apps: all included

Final numbers must be validated against infrastructure cost.

## Pricing model

Piggles $49/mo
Extra 10,000 customers $10/mo
Extra 5,000 email sends $10/mo
Extra 10 GB storage $5/mo
Extra site $10/mo
Extra location $19/mo
Extra team member $5/mo

## Expansion

Possible add-ons:

- additional user
- additional location
- additional site
- storage blocks
- email send blocks
- high contact volume

## Avoid

Do not launch with Basic/Pro/Enterprise comparison grids, paid CRM/CMS/bookings unlocks, or product-count traps such as "11th product requires upgrade".

## App enablement

App enabled = workspace preference.  
Entitled app = product capability.  
For standard Piggles subscription, normal Piggles apps should be entitled.
