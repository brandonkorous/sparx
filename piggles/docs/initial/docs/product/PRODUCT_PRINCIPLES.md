# Product Principles

1. **Simple does not mean basic.**
2. **People should think in business tasks, not software categories.**
3. **Power should appear when needed, not all at once.**
4. **The workbench is a strength.**
5. **Do not make the customer pay extra just to turn on an app.**
6. **Charge for scale, not comprehension.**
7. **Defaults should be opinionated and reversible.**
8. **Serious work deserves serious behavior.**
9. **Playfulness belongs in the brand, not in every label.**
10. **Shared capability should stay shared under the hood.**
11. **Piggles must be allowed to win.**
12. **Avoid decisions that make the Piggles-vs-Sparx market test unfair.**
