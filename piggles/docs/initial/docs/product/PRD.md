# Product Requirements Document — Piggles

## 1. Product summary

Piggles is a full-power business management platform for people who need to run a business, not learn enterprise software.

Piggles uses an MDI workbench so customers can work across related business tasks at the same time: editing a site while previewing it, managing a product while working on inventory, preparing an email while viewing customers, and so on.

Piggles should be powerful enough to become the primary product if the Piggles brand outperforms Sparx.

## 2. Positioning

**Tagline:** Business software for people who have a business to run.

Piggles is approachable, capable, opinionated, flexible when needed, visually memorable, progressively disclosed, and priced simply.

Piggles is not a toy, beginner-only app, watered-down edition, bundle of separately priced modules, or a joke/pun-first product.

## 3. Target customers

Primary: solo operators, local service businesses, small retail, makers, food businesses, creators, beauty professionals, tattoo artists, trades, consultants, and small teams.

Secondary: growing small businesses that need more sophistication but still value a humane interface.

## 4. Core jobs to be done

Customers should be able to:

- create and manage a public site;
- sell products and services;
- manage customers;
- manage bookings;
- track stock;
- send messages/email;
- create content;
- invoice customers;
- understand money and business activity;
- manage a small team;
- automate recurring work;
- connect external services;
- use advanced capability without being forced to understand internal platform terminology.

## 5. Product surfaces

### meetpiggles.com

Public marketing, content, SEO, pricing, docs/help.

### getpiggles.com

Account creation, authentication, onboarding, provisioning, billing, invites.

### mypiggles.com

Authenticated MDI workbench.

## 6. Workbench requirements

The workbench must:

- support multiple open applications/panels;
- support move/resize/minimize/restore;
- remember workspace layout;
- support favorites and recent items;
- support global search/command;
- support deep links to an app/entity;
- support contextual cross-app actions;
- support responsive fallback behavior;
- support task-oriented launchers.

## 7. Simplicity model

Piggles simplifies through plain-language app names, opinionated defaults, onboarding, task-oriented entry points, progressive disclosure, contextual help, curated initial workspace, and saved state.

Piggles does not simplify primarily through feature removal.

## 8. Pricing

Public target:

- $49/month;
- all normal Piggles apps included;
- one business;
- one initial location;
- one primary site;
- 3 users included;
- capacity limits for cost-driving resources;
- optional capacity expansion.

## 9. App enablement

Apps can be enabled/disabled per workspace for cleanliness. Enabling an app does not change subscription price. It may initialize defaults, add workspace entries, and trigger contextual setup help.

## 10. Advanced features

Advanced functionality should use progressive disclosure:

1. standard flow;
2. More options;
3. Advanced.

Developer/technical concepts should only surface in explicitly technical contexts.

## 11. Success metrics

Activation:

- completed onboarding;
- first site published;
- first product/service created;
- first customer added;
- first booking/order/invoice;
- first 7-day return.

Engagement:

- weekly active businesses;
- active apps per business;
- MDI multi-window usage;
- task completion;
- retained workspaces.

Commercial:

- trial-to-paid;
- monthly churn;
- expansion via capacity;
- support burden;
- payment-failure recovery.

Brand experiment:

- conversion rate vs Sparx;
- direct/organic brand search;
- unaided recall;
- signup completion;
- retention by acquisition brand.

## 12. Non-goals for first release

- complicated enterprise packaging;
- arbitrary feature gating;
- aggressive pig puns;
- separate duplicated backends where shared services are practical;
- forcing migration from Sparx before Piggles earns it.
