# Sparx / Piggles Coexistence and Migration Strategy

## Current strategy

Do not decide the winner before the market does.

Allow Sparx to continue with modular positioning/pricing and Piggles to launch with flat pricing, human terminology, and a distinct brand while sharing services where practical.

## If Piggles wins

Possible future:

- Piggles becomes the flagship;
- Sparx becomes advanced/developer, legacy, or is retired;
- existing Sparx tenants receive a supported migration or UI-switch path.

## If Sparx wins

Piggles can remain an alternate SMB brand, narrow to a segment, or be retired without losing shared platform investment.

## Migration readiness

Prefer product-neutral business IDs and shared tenant data. Track product origin/current experience separately where useful.

Do not force a data migration merely to switch UI/brand if the same business data can render through another shell.
