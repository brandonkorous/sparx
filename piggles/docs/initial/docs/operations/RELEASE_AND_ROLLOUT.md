# Release and Rollout

## Alpha

Internal/invited. Validate shell, auth, onboarding, MDI, and terminology.

## Private beta

Use real small businesses. Observe onboarding, support questions, and actual app usage.

## Public beta

Use $49 pricing, limited marketing, and compare Piggles acquisition against Sparx.

## GA

Public marketing, stable capacity enforcement, support/docs ready, incident processes ready.

## Feature flags

Use flags for product shell, navigation changes, pricing model, app exposure, advanced controls, and capacity enforcement.

## Kill switches

Maintain ability to disable a broken app, new signups, provisioning, or a specific integration path.
