## What happened

Her empty Campaigns list said a campaign is "somebody finds your page, leaves their
details, and eventually buys something or books you in." The Marrow Review sells
nothing and takes no bookings. What she wants from a reader is a sign-up.

## The fix

Both consoles' `surfaces/funnels/campaigns.tsx`: "...leaves their details, and in the end
does what you were hoping for: signs up, books in or buys." True for a journal, a salon
and a shop alike. Nothing reads the sentence.
