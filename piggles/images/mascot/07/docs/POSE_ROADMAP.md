# Batch 07 Pose Roadmap

The batch establishes a reusable static scene pattern for small-business verticals:

1. Piggles performs one recognizable task.
2. A compact workstation or service fixture establishes the setting.
3. One to three secondary props reinforce the business category.
4. The charcoal shirt and official P remain visible.
5. The complete vignette stays readable at reduced sizes.

Future scene batches can extend this system into hospitality, pet services, repair trades, events, agriculture, professional services, and mobile businesses without changing Piggles' base proportions or wardrobe.
