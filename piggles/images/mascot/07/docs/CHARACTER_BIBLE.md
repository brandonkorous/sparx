# Batch 07 Character Bible

Batch 07 follows `MASCOT.md` v1.1 without introducing a new Piggles variant.

## Locked character traits

- One extremely round head-and-body mass with no visible neck
- Natural soft peach-pink body tone
- Slightly richer pink snout and inner ears
- Small black oval eyes and two vertical nostrils
- Tiny arms, legs, hooves, and tail
- Plain charcoal short-sleeve shirt
- Official Piggles pink P centered on the chest
- Friendly, capable expression rather than exaggerated excitement

## Scene rules

- Piggles remains the visual focus.
- Business context comes from a compact environment and a small set of recognizable props.
- Props use cream, charcoal, warm gray, natural wood, restrained Piggles pink, and necessary natural product colors.
- Clothing is not replaced by a role costume. A single functional cap or lower apron is acceptable when the shirt and P remain visible.
- All PNG and WebP scene assets have transparent backgrounds.
