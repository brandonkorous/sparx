## Naming convention

`piggles-<semantic-action>.png`

Examples:

- `piggles-message.png`
- `piggles-package.png`
- `piggles-chart-up.png`

Avoid naming by visual appearance such as:

- `pig-03.png`
- `happy-pig.png`
- `pink-pig-2.png`

Semantic names make dynamic selection stable even if artwork is replaced later.
