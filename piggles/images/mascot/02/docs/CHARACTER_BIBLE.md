# Piggles Character Bible

Piggles is a warm, competent, almost-circular natural peach-pink pig rendered as a soft dimensional commercial-animation character. The character is helpful without feeling corporate and playful without becoming chaotic.

## Canonical traits

- Body and head flow together with no visible neck; 70–80% of visual mass is the round body.
- Natural soft peach-pink skin, richer natural-pink snout and inner ears.
- Tiny legs, muted dark-brown hooves, small rounded arms, subtle tail.
- Small black oval eyes with minimal white, friendly mouth, and two vertical nostrils.
- Plain matte charcoal short-sleeve shirt with the official Piggles pink `P`.
- Soft dimensional shading, minimal texture, clean silhouette, transparent background.

## Consistency rules

Keep anatomy, color family, eye treatment, snout proportions, hoof style, shirt, official mark, and lighting stable. Props should use charcoal, warm gray, cream, kraft, restrained Piggles pink, and intentional yellow only for role/status accents. Avoid candy-pink skin, large white eyes, photoreal skin, glossy plastic, busy scenery, costume changes, cropped anatomy, and generated logo redesigns.

## Batch 02 expression

This set emphasizes useful interaction states: guidance, mobile activity, delivery, approval, tasks, analytics, onboarding, messages, and discovery.
