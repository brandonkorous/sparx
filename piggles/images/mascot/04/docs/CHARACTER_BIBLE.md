# Piggles Character Bible — Batch 04

Batch 04 uses the branded canonical Piggles system exclusively. Piggles remains extremely round, with one continuous peach-pink head-and-body silhouette, tiny limbs, dark brown hooves, compact ears, small black oval eyes, a broad snout with two vertical nostrils, and a friendly capable expression.

Every scene requires the plain charcoal short-sleeve shirt and the official pink snout-bearing Piggles `P`, centered and readable on the chest. The setting must never hide or replace the mascot identity.

Environment pieces use cream, charcoal, warm gray, pale natural wood, and restrained Piggles pink. Scenes stay compact, clean, and secondary to Piggles. Avoid complete rooms, busy backgrounds, arbitrary bright colors, costume changes, extra characters, generic replacement logos, and realistic human-scale anatomy.
