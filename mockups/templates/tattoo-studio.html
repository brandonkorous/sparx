How a tattoo gets made here.

Every custom piece starts with a conversation. No surprise pricing, no rushed line — just a clear path from idea to healed skin.

01

Consult

Share your reference, placement and size. We talk it through in person or over video and match you to the right artist — free, no obligation.

02

Design & deposit

A $100 deposit reserves your date and goes toward the final cost. Your artist draws a custom piece; we refine it together before the day.

03

Session & aftercare

Show up rested and fed. We tattoo in a private, single-use, fully licensed station and send you home with a written aftercare plan.

A quiet room, a steady hand, and time to do it right.

Ironleaf opened in 2011 with one rule that hasn't changed: nobody leaves with a tattoo they'll outgrow. We'd rather talk you into the right piece than rush you through the wrong one.

The space is small on purpose — three stations, no music too loud to think over, and a sketchbook for every client. We tattoo people who want their ink to mean something in twenty years, not twenty likes.

— Mara & the Ironleaf crew
