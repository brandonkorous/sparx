We opened the studio we always wished existed.

Maren & Wilde began with two chairs, a stubborn idea, and a long lease on a corner full of afternoon light. We wanted unhurried appointments, honest advice, and a room that smelled of eucalyptus and good coffee — a place where the work is exacting but the feeling is easy.

Twelve years on, the idea hasn't changed. Small team, real conversation, and color and care that look like you on your best ordinary day.

— Maren & Theo
