Modules come first — and steer everything after
Capabilities are an explicit choice, not a side effect of the template — and that choice filters the catalog.
With hundreds–thousands of blueprints, modules are the primary filter: pick "Sell + Email" and the gallery narrows to templates that fit. Your selection drives what's activated and billed; the template installs real content within it. A blueprint's requiresModules flips from the trigger to a compatibility filter (show only templates whose needs ⊆ your pick). À-la-carte curation still lives in the dashboard switchboard, after launch.

Billing lifecycle

trial → payment → pause → suspend — and back, anytime

The plan from step 1 is a Stripe subscription created at module-select — trialing, with no card. The owner can always reach the dashboard to add one; the locks below only ever apply to the public site, and nothing is deleted along the way.

Day 0 · trial starts
Subscription created — no card

One subscription, one line item per active module (toggling later adds/removes items, prorated). trial_period_days: 14, no payment method. Everything's on.

Days 7 · 12 · 14 · heads-up
Countdown — add a payment method

Dashboard banner + emails: "3 days left — add a card to keep Builder, Commerce, CMS." Nothing changes yet.

Day 14 · trial ends — it forks
Card on file → active
Subscription goes live

First invoice = the sum of active modules, one bill. Business as usual.

No card → modules pause
Dashboard features gate

Paid modules show "add payment to reactivate." The public site stays live — grace begins.

Days 14–21 · grace (~7 days)
Site stays live; we keep nudging

Visitors see the site normally; the owner gets daily reactivation prompts. A lapsed active subscription (failed renewal → Stripe retries + dunning) lands in this same grace state.

Day 21 · site paused
Public site shows a friendly, non-bypassable overlay

No active subscription past grace → the storefront serves a full-page "unavailable" screen that can't be dismissed. The dashboard stays open so the owner can add a card; data is retained.

Catching a fresh spark This site will be back in a flash.
Anytime · reactivate
Add a card → restored instantly

Modules switch back on and the overlay lifts. The subscription resumes from its retained items — no rebuild, no data loss.

Decisions to confirm

three open calls before I wire it in
Decision A
Physical address in onboarding?
It means different things per module and a CMS/CRM-only tenant has no use for it. Forcing it up front taxes the 5-minute goal.
Recommend → omit; capture contextually later
Decision B
Site-name default label?
Signup creates the primary site named "Default". Your note suggested "Primary." The editor pre-fills whatever we pick.
Your call → "Default" vs "Primary"
Decision C
Editable tenant slug in onboarding?
Pre-launch it's safe to rename, but it needs a real tenant-slug rename op (update tenant + primary host). Read-only is simpler.
Recommend → editable (build rename op)
