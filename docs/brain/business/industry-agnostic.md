The platform is **not commerce- or industry-specific.** Never default to a single vertical as the running example or lens.

- **Gillett Diesel Service** is the first Enterprise client (B2B + fleet + MCP requirements), and it drives the *initial* feature set — but Gillett is **one client, not the frame**. Vary verticals in examples and seed data; never make diesel / auto-parts the default.

**Why:** an industry-agnostic platform described through one industry's lens ships that industry's assumptions into the product.

**How to apply:** in examples, demos, seed data, and copy, spread across verticals (a bakery, a law firm, a publisher, a distributor). Treat Gillett as a client with requirements, not the shape of the product.
