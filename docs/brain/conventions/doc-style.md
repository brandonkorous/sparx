Every long-form doc starts with `Version`, `Author`, `Last Updated`. Bump the version + date on material edits. Author is **Brandon Korous**. Dates are absolute ISO (e.g. `2026-07-03`), never relative.

- **The brain is the map over the docs.** New *durable* knowledge goes into an atomic, linked brain note; the long-form `docs/NN` are references being slimmed graduated-per-node (see [[tasks]]).
- **No competitor names in shipped artifacts** — describe patterns in our own language. (Naming references in conversation is fine.)
